Meet Gill Shannon

Experience built on both sides of the partnership

Gill Shannon has spent more than 35 years in luxury travel, with senior experience across leisure sales, product, marketing and business development. She understands how hotels and tour operators each define commercial success, and how to make the two work together.

Her career spans JA Resorts & Hotels, Travelbeam Luxury Hotels, InterContinental Hotels Group, Jumeirah Hotels & Resorts, Address Hotels + Resorts, ITC Travel Group and Elegant Resorts — hotel-side commercial roles alongside tour-operator product experience.

That combination underpins a deep familiarity with the UK and Ireland travel market and long-standing relationships across the trade.

We believe representation should be personal, strategic and deliver results.
